<Update label="June 2026" description="Model selection, yearly plans, streaming for all paid users" tags={["Music AI", "Pricing", "Streaming"]}>
  ## Model selection & yearly plans

  - **Model Selection** added to Audio Generation — choose from `v6`, `v6-pro`, `v7`, or `v7-pro` with the `model` parameter. See [Music AI v2](/api-documentation/conversions/musicaistreaming).
  - **Streaming** is now available for **all paid users** on `v6-pro` and `v7-pro`.
  - **Yearly Plan** is now available — the most affordable option. Yearly plan members retain the current per-song rates.
  - Starting **August 1**, monthly plan pricing will be updated. [Subscribe to a yearly plan](/api-documentation/index/pricing) to lock in current rates.
  - Updated pricing for **Music AI**, **Remix**, **Inpaint**, **Extend**, and **Sing Over Instrumental**. See [Pricing](/api-documentation/index/pricing).
  - Deprecated **Music AI v1** — please migrate to [Music AI v2](/api-documentation/conversions/musicaistreaming).
</Update>

<Update label="May 2026" description="Extraction eco mode, optional cover images, streaming webhook" tags={["Extraction", "Webhooks"]}>
  - **Extraction** endpoint updated — added `eco_mode` to support faster parallel extraction.
  - Added a **title** field to the Audio Generation feature.
  - Cover images are now **optional** in Audio Generation — enable it to receive a cover image along with the audio.
  - Added a **Streaming URL** webhook. Music AI v2 now provides 7 webhooks.
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026" description="Image to Song, output count control, file retention" tags={["New feature", "Pricing"]}>
  - **Image to Song** feature introduced.
  - New parameter to control the **number of song outputs** in Inpaint and Extend.
  - Retention time: **MP3 files are stored for 90 days**, **WAV files for 7 days**.
  - **WAV files** are sent in webhooks again starting March 24, 2026.
  - Pricing updated for **Inpaint**, **Extend**, and **Custom Voice Music Generator**.
</Update>

<Update label="January 2026" description="Lyrics replacement, song length control, SDK, quality upgrades" tags={["Music AI", "SDK"]}>
  - **Lyrics section replacement** added to Extend and Inpaint — specify the lyrics you want to add or replace.
  - **Song length control** added to Music AI.
  - **Streaming** available for enterprise users on [Music AI v2](/api-documentation/conversions/musicaistreaming).
  - **Single-song generation** available for enterprise users.
  - **SDK and tools** added to the [documentation](/api-documentation/index/sdk).
  - Remix, Inpaint, Extend, and Sing Over Instrumental upgraded for **higher accuracy**.
  - Improved Music AI output **quality**.
  - **Cover image** now sent with Remix, Inpaint, Extend, and Sing Over Instrumental.
  - **No WAV file** sent in webhooks starting **February 9**.
</Update>
